About MyTimeline

Mytimeline is your personal event organizer, designed to help you keep track of all your important moments and milestones. With a clean, user-friendly interface, you can easily add, view, and manage your events in a single, chronological timeline.

This website uses Firebase Firestore for real-time data storage, ensuring your events are always up to date across your devices. It also incorporates the Gemini API to provide creative event descriptions and planning ideas, helping you stay organized and inspired.
